Transparency matters. This page explains how affiliate links may support Synth Visual and how we aim to keep our editorial content useful, clear, and honest.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Our disclosure

How affiliate links work

Some Synth Visual articles may contain affiliate links. If you click one of these links and make a qualifying purchase or complete another eligible action, we may receive a commission or referral fee. This does not add an extra cost to you.

Affiliate relationships can help support the time and resources required to research, write, update, and maintain this website. Not every link is an affiliate link, and we may discuss products or services without receiving compensation.

Editorial judgment comes first

Our goal is to publish clear, practical information that helps readers understand technology and make informed decisions. A potential commission should not be treated as proof that a product or service is right for every reader, and compensation does not guarantee a positive recommendation.

We aim to explain relevant benefits, limitations, and factors worth considering. Where an article is based on public documentation and research rather than hands-on testing, it should not imply that Synth Visual personally tested the product or service.

Disclosures near recommendations

This page provides general information about our affiliate practices. When an article contains affiliate links, we aim to make the relationship clear near the relevant recommendation or link as well, so readers do not have to find this page to understand that a commission may be earned.

Prices, features, and availability

Product details, subscription prices, promotions, features, and terms can change. Always confirm current information with the provider before making a purchase. We aim to keep our content useful, but we cannot guarantee that every third-party page or offer will remain unchanged after publication.

Questions about this disclosure

If you have a question about this page or about Synth Visual's editorial approach, visit our About page, where site and contact information is provided.

A note about transparency: An affiliate relationship does not mean a recommendation is automatically suitable for you. Consider your own needs, review the provider's current terms and privacy policy, and compare available alternatives before deciding.
