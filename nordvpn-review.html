What Happens After the Promotional Period?

NordVPN subscriptions automatically renew unless you turn off automatic renewal before the next billing date.

The important distinction is between the introductory price and the renewal price. The first applies only to the initial subscription period. Subsequent charges use the renewal price applicable at that time, which may change.

Consider Basic. The two-year promotion reviewed for this article costs $94.23 for 27 months. Its advertised renewal price is $139.08 per year. When the introductory period ends, the annual renewal price—not the original $3.49 monthly equivalent—becomes the important figure to consider.

NordVPN states that upcoming subscription periods are charged in advance. Depending on the billing cycle, a renewal payment may be collected annually, potentially up to 14 days before renewal, or monthly. The company also states that customers receive a notification email before a charge for subscriptions longer than one month.

Taxes may apply in addition to the displayed price. Review the price, renewal period, and applicable terms at checkout rather than relying solely on a promotional banner.

For the current conditions, consult NordVPN's Terms of Service and the subscription disclosure on its pricing page.

NordVPN Features Worth Understanding

VPN encryption and IP address masking

A VPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server. Websites that see your connection generally see the VPN server's public IP address rather than your original public IP address.

This can help protect traffic from observation on local networks and change the apparent location of your connection. It does not make you completely anonymous. Websites can still recognize accounts you sign into, and cookies or other tracking techniques may continue to identify your activity.

NordLynx and performance

NordLynx is NordVPN's protocol built around WireGuard technology, with NordVPN's own implementation intended to combine efficient connections with additional privacy protections.

Protocol choice can affect speed, latency, and connection stability, but no protocol guarantees maximum performance in every situation. Your location, server load, device, internet provider, and distance to the selected server all influence the result.

A June 2026 performance report from Artifact Security tested five VPN providers across five origin regions and multiple fixed routes. The report placed NordVPN first for average download speed in its Windows route-pinned leaderboard. That is useful evidence for the specific conditions tested, not proof that NordVPN is always the fastest VPN for every person. The report is hosted on NordVPN's content-delivery domain, so its methodology, publisher, and testing scope deserve consideration alongside its results.

Scam, phishing, and malware protection

Scam and phishing protection helps block connections to certain malicious domains. The higher-tier malware tools add functions such as scanning downloaded files and checking applications for security weaknesses on supported desktop platforms.

These tools supplement, rather than replace, operating-system updates, careful browsing, and good security practices.

Ad and tracker blocking

The included ad and tracker blocker in the higher plans is intended to reduce intrusive advertising and tracking. The detailed plan information lists this feature as desktop-only, so check compatibility before paying extra for it.

Email threat protection is also limited by supported services and platforms; the published offer identifies Gmail and Yahoo on desktop. Feature availability should not be assumed to be identical across mobile and desktop apps.

Dark Web Monitor and Dark Web Monitor Pro

Dark Web Monitor can monitor up to five email addresses for potential data exposure and issue alerts when it detects a match. Dark Web Monitor Pro extends that capability to up to eight email addresses and supports additional information types, including credit cards, national identification or Social Security numbers, and phone numbers in selected countries.

Monitoring can alert you to exposed data, but it cannot guarantee that every breach will be detected or prevent someone from misusing information that has already leaked.

Password management and encrypted cloud storage

Complete and Prime include NordPass password management and 1 TB of encrypted cloud storage in the U.S.-dollar offer reviewed. These are separate functions from the VPN itself.

A password manager helps organize and secure login credentials. Encrypted cloud storage is designed to protect stored files. Neither service should be treated as an automatic consequence of having an ordinary VPN connection; these benefits depend on the subscription bundle.

Dedicated IP

A Dedicated IP provides a personal, fixed VPN IP address rather than one shared with other users. It may reduce some CAPTCHA challenges and can help with services that expect a stable IP address.

NordVPN lists Dedicated IP as an optional paid add-on, not a standard feature included automatically with every Basic, Complete, or Prime subscription.

How Many Devices Can You Use with NordVPN?

NordVPN's support documentation confirms up to 10 simultaneous device connections per account.

There is a condition when several devices connect to the same server: they must use different supported VPN protocols. The support page explains that up to five connections can use the same server at once under its protocol restrictions.

If you need protection for more devices, you can configure NordVPN on a compatible router. A router uses one device slot while protecting devices connected through that network, subject to the router's capabilities and configuration.

NordVPN offers apps for major desktop and mobile platforms, as well as browser extensions. Check its official apps and features page for current compatibility.

Does NordVPN Offer a Free Trial?

NordVPN's current support page lists a seven-day free trial for new customers in eligible countries, including the United States, on selected one-year and two-year plans. The offer is not available to every customer or with every plan and payment method.

The trial terms reviewed require an accepted payment method, such as a credit card or PayPal. No payment is collected during the seven-day trial, but the chosen subscription begins automatically at the displayed price if you do not cancel before the trial ends.

An official U.S.-dollar pricing campaign reviewed for Prime showed a separate trial flow: $0 upfront, followed by a charge of $202.23 for 27 months unless the customer cancels first. A one-year Prime trial offer showed $0 upfront, followed by $113.88 for 12 months. These are campaign-specific checkout terms, not a promise that every visitor will see the same offer.

NordVPN also documents a separate three-day trial for first-time Android users who subscribe through Google Play. That trial follows Google Play's terms and cancellation process.

The free-trial rules are separate from the 30-day money-back guarantee. Check NordVPN's current free-trial conditions before starting.

NordVPN's refund policy

NordVPN offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for eligible initial subscription purchases. The current refund guidance says a customer can request a refund within 30 days of the first purchase by contacting customer support.

The standard guarantee does not cover a renewed subscription, unless applicable law provides otherwise. Purchases through third-party app stores can follow separate refund processes: Apple App Store purchases must be addressed through Apple, while Amazon Appstore purchases require contacting Amazon.

A refund is not automatic simply because you cancel. Read the current NordVPN refund policy for the process and conditions.

Is NordVPN Worth It?

NordVPN may be worth paying for if you need VPN protection across several devices, want access to a broad server network, or would benefit from the extra security tools included in a higher-tier subscription.

Basic is the logical starting point if your main priority is VPN protection and you do not need the bundled password manager or cloud storage.

Complete is worth comparing if you would actually use its malware protection, ad and tracker blocking, email and call/message protection, password management, cloud storage, and enhanced monitoring.

Prime is most relevant if its identity and financial monitoring features are useful to you and available in your location. Its additional price is harder to justify when you only want a conventional VPN connection.

The upfront commitment is another major consideration. The two-year offer has lower introductory monthly-equivalent prices, but it requires a larger payment and renews at a higher annual price. Monthly billing costs more per month but avoids committing to a lengthy initial subscription.

NordVPN can also be unnecessary for some people. A VPN does not eliminate every tracking method, prevent every cyberattack, or replace antivirus and other security measures in all circumstances. The right choice depends on what you want to protect and which features you actually need.

Using NordVPN for Streaming and Across Devices

NordVPN supports desktop and mobile apps, including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS, along with selected browser extensions. A compatible router is another option for covering devices that do not run the app directly.

A VPN can change the public IP address and apparent connection location seen by a streaming service. However, it does not guarantee access to a particular streaming library or title. Streaming platforms apply their own licensing arrangements, account restrictions, and technical controls, and access can change over time.

NordVPN's server directory currently advertises coverage across 225 locations. Exact server counts and available locations can change, so use the current directory rather than relying on an older promotional figure.

Frequently Asked Questions About NordVPN Cost

1. How much does NordVPN cost per month?

The U.S.-dollar offer reviewed lists monthly subscriptions at $14.99 for Basic, $19.99 for Complete, and $29.99 for Prime. Longer subscriptions advertise lower monthly-equivalent prices, but they require an upfront payment.

2. Is NordVPN cheaper with a one-year or two-year subscription?

The two-year promotional offer has the lower advertised monthly equivalent in the pricing reviewed. The one-year plan requires less upfront commitment, so the best choice depends on your budget and expected length of use.

3. Does NordVPN charge the advertised monthly price every month?

Not necessarily. For a long-term offer, the advertised monthly amount can be an equivalent price calculated from a prepaid introductory term. Check the total charge and billing period.

4. How much does NordVPN cost after the introductory offer?

The reviewed annual renewal prices are $139.08 for Basic, $219.48 for Complete, and $296.28 for Prime. Renewal prices are subject to change, so confirm the current terms.

5. Does NordVPN have a free trial?

NordVPN currently documents a seven-day trial for new customers in selected countries and eligible plans, as well as a separate three-day Google Play trial for first-time Android users. Eligibility and automatic-conversion terms apply.

6. Does NordVPN offer a 30-day money-back guarantee?

Yes, for eligible initial subscription purchases. The standard guarantee does not normally cover renewed subscriptions, and app-store purchases may have separate refund procedures.

7. How many devices can use one NordVPN account?

Up to 10 devices can connect simultaneously. A compatible router can protect additional devices through the router's connection, using one device slot.

8. What is the difference between Basic, Complete, and Prime?

Basic focuses on VPN protection and core included features. Complete adds a wider set of security tools, password management, cloud storage, and enhanced monitoring. Prime adds selected identity and financial monitoring benefits, subject to eligibility and plan terms.

9. Is NordVPN worth the price?

It can be worthwhile if you need its VPN service or will use the additional features in your chosen plan. It may be poor value if you only need a basic VPN but pay for services you never use.

10. Does NordVPN offer discounts or coupons?

The official pricing page displays promotional subscription prices, and some campaigns include extra months or a trial. I did not verify a separate universal coupon code. Check the current offer and checkout terms rather than relying on an old coupon listing.

Conclusion

NordVPN's introductory pricing ranges from $3.49 per month equivalent for Basic to $7.49 for Prime in the two-year U.S.-dollar promotion reviewed. The more important figures are the upfront charge, the duration of the introductory period, and the renewal price.

Basic is the most straightforward choice for users primarily seeking VPN protection. Complete may provide better value for people who need its bundled security tools, while Prime makes the most sense when its additional identity-related features are relevant and eligible.

Before subscribing, compare the actual features included in your region, verify the checkout total, and decide whether the renewal price is acceptable. To compare the available offers, visit NordVPN's official pricing page and review the exact terms shown before completing your purchase.

The main takeaway

Don't judge NordVPN by its lowest advertised monthly equivalent alone. Compare the upfront cost, how long the promotional price lasts, what the plan actually includes, and the price at renewal.
